Drop-off inquiries

Ask before you load up.

Drop-offs at 6442 University Ave, San Diego are available by scheduled appointment only. Contact our crew to confirm your materials and arrange a time before you arrive.

How to arrange a drop-off

  1. Text photos, the material type, and approximate quantity.
  2. Confirm accepted materials and any fees, then schedule your appointment at 6442 University Ave.
  3. Arrive only at your confirmed appointment time. Unscheduled drop-offs are not accepted.

What materials can you bring?

Acceptance depends on the material and available arrangements. Send photos for confirmation. We do not accept hazardous materials such as asbestos, liquid paint, propane tanks, motor oil, chemicals, or biohazardous waste.
